## Reading these numbers

- **Headline.** Every fine-tune scores above base. qv_r8, qv_r4, qv_r16, attn_r8 are nominally significant (p < 0.05). After Holm correction over the 5 comparisons with base, qv_r8, qv_r16 remain significant. Resampling whole databases instead of questions, the 95% CI excludes zero only for qv_r16.
- **One seed per config.** Seed-to-seed variance is not measured, so part of any gap between two fine-tunes may be training noise rather than the config.
- **Fine-tuning reshuffles which questions are right far more than it raises the total.** Every run gains 74–84 questions over base and loses 43–58. 707 questions are right in all six evals and 145 in none.
- **Base is strong and has probably seen Spider.** Untuned, it writes Spider's gold spacing (`Country ,  COUNT(*)` with the double space) in 31 of its 1034 predictions (3.0%), a formatting quirk with no other obvious source, and 4 of those are character-for-character the gold query. So Qwen2.5-Coder likely saw Spider in pretraining, which caps the headroom fine-tuning can show.
- **The column-order check.** The harness compares columns positionally, which is stricter than the official test-suite eval. Allowing any column order raises every run by 2.3–3.8 points and helps base by 3.8, so the strict Δ is the larger estimate of the fine-tuning gain. Strict: qv_r16 79.88, qv_r8 79.11, attn_r8 78.24, all7_r8 78.24, qv_r4 78.14, base 75.92. Any column order: qv_r16 82.59, qv_r8 81.91, attn_r8 81.14, qv_r4 80.95, all7_r8 80.56, base 79.69.
- **Wins and losses, counted.** Of the 74–84 questions a run wins from base, 10–20 are ones where base's query did not execute, and 15–19 are ones where base returned the right rows with its columns in another order than the gold. Of the 43–58 it loses, 4 are right up to column order, and 81–89% execute and return a wrong result.
- **What changes in the SQL, counted over all predictions.** `INTERSECT`: gold 3.9%, base 2.1%, fine-tunes 3.7–4.2%. `LIKE`: gold 1.4%, base 1.5%, fine-tunes 1.3%. Mean JOINs per query: gold 0.50, base 0.39, fine-tunes 0.48–0.50. Spider's gold spacing: gold 75%, base 3%, fine-tunes 77.1–77.6%. `no such column` errors: base 37, fine-tunes 16–25.
- **Where the gain sits: medium.** Net of won − lost vs base, the runs gain +16 to +27 questions on medium and +2 to +14 on easy, hard and extra combined. Extra has the most churn: 22–26% of its questions flip either way (against 13–14% on medium and 4–5% on easy), yet its net change is only -1 to +5 questions. Easy is near ceiling: base scores 91.5% there and 56.0% on extra.
- **Empty gold results.** 53 gold queries return nothing, and the models "match" 39–45 of them. On the other 981 questions the gain over base is +1.83–+3.77 points, against +2.22–+3.97 on all 1034.
