# Adapter-scaling probe

Generated by `scripts/summarize_scaling.py`. `qv_r16` (the best adapter on Spider) is scored with its LoRA scaling `alpha / r` multiplied by a factor below 1 at inference (`--lora-scale`), with no retraining. Scale 0 is the base model and scale 1 the adapter as trained; both rows are the committed main results. Scaled runs: Spider 0.25, 0.5; BIRD 0.25, 0.5. Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct, nf4 + bf16 compute, greedy decoding (repetition penalty 1.0, i.e. off).

This is a diagnostic, not a tuned result: both benchmarks are dev sets, so picking a scale from these tables would be tuning on dev. "95% CI" resamples questions and "by database" resamples whole databases (5,000 resamples, seed 0). p-values are exact two-sided McNemar, unadjusted. "Share of the full adapter's Δ" is Δ vs base at that scale divided by Δ vs base at scale 1; it is a ratio of two noisy numbers.

- **At 0.25×:** Spider +0.97 points vs base [-0.48, +2.51], 24% of the full adapter's change. BIRD -0.13 [-1.56, +1.24], 3% of the full adapter's change.
- **At 0.5×:** Spider +2.71 points vs base [+0.97, +4.55], 68% of the full adapter's change. BIRD -0.98 [-2.74, +0.78], 26% of the full adapter's change.
- **Trade-off.** At 0.5× the Spider gain is still distinguishable from zero (question-level 95% CI above zero) and the BIRD change is not (its CI includes zero). The interval including zero does not show that the loss is gone, only that this sample cannot tell it from none.
- **Resampling whole databases instead of questions,** no tested scale has a Spider interval above zero together with a BIRD interval that includes zero (0.25×: Spider [-1.11, +3.32], BIRD [-1.90, +1.57]; 0.5×: Spider [-0.20, +5.77], BIRD [-4.32, +2.34]). As trained: Spider [+0.82, +7.56], BIRD [-7.92, +0.18].
- **Spider moves one way with scale:** Δ vs base is +0.97 at 0.25×, +2.71 at 0.5×, +3.97 at 1×.
- **BIRD moves one way with scale:** Δ vs base is -0.13 at 0.25×, -0.98 at 0.5×, -3.78 at 1×.
- **Against the adapter as trained** (paired, unadjusted p): at 0.25× Spider -3.00 points (+29 / −60, p 0.001) and BIRD +3.65 (+132 / −76, p 1.3e-04); at 0.5× Spider -1.26 points (+20 / −33, p 0.098) and BIRD +2.80 (+99 / −56, p 6.9e-04).

The BIRD accuracy change at 0.5× is 26% of the trained one. These are counts of surface patterns; they show what moves with the accuracy, not what causes it.
